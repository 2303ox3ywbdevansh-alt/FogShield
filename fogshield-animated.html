🚨 The Critical Problem

Mining Operations in Fog = Catastrophic Risk

  • Zero Visibility Blindness: Dense monsoon fog and iron ore dust render cameras useless
  • Road Straying & Roll-offs: Dumpers lose track when haul roads disappear in fog
  • Collision Risks: Blind spots and occluded intersections cause deadly accidents
  • Operational Slowdowns: Frequent halts and fog shutdowns cripple productivity
🫥 🌫️

✨ FogShield: The Game Changer

🛡️

Perception Through ANY Condition

  • Multi-Sensor Fusion: LiDAR + Thermal IR + 77GHz Radar + High-Precision DGPS = Zero-visibility perception
  • In-Cab AR Guidance: Dynamic virtual road boundaries and safe corridor overlays on driver HUD
  • V2X Communication: Detects approaching vehicles from blind spots using V2V/V2I mesh network
  • Edge AI Processing: 100% locally on-vehicle—zero cloud latency, works offline

⚡ Powerful Features

🎯

Unified Perception

Combines multi-angle hazard avoidance with active visual road path overlays in a single system

Mining-Specific Brake AI

Factors heavy dumper payload, stopping inertia, surface friction, and road slope into risk scoring

Sub-Second Autonomy

High-speed edge inference providing safety interventions in less than 100ms without internet

Security-First Design

Custom V2X Protocol & Quad Encryption for secure vehicle-to-infrastructure mesh

👁️

Zero-Visibility Perception

Thermal IR and Radar pierce dense fog and dust where RGB cameras completely fail

🛣️

Lane Keep Assist

Digital lane overlays keep dumpers strictly on-track when physical roads disappear

⚙️ Advanced Technology Stack

🔧 Hardware Arsenal

  • LiDAR: Velodyne Puck Lite (64-ch, 100m range, 360° FOV)
  • Thermal IR: FLIR Boson (fog-proof vehicle detection)
  • Radar: Continental ARS430 (77GHz, velocity tracking, 200m)
  • DGPS: Novatel PwrPak7 (cm-level accuracy, RTK-capable)
  • Edge AI: NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin (275 TOPS, 64GB RAM, real-time)

💻 Software Stack

  • ML/AI: PyTorch, YOLOv8, TensorFlow Lite, Scikit-learn
  • Sensor Fusion: ROS 2, OpenCV, NumPy, Apache Spark
  • Backend: TensorRT, Apache Kafka, PostgreSQL, InfluxDB
  • Frontend: Flask/FastAPI, React.js, D3.js, Three.js
  • DevOps: Docker, Kubernetes, Jenkins, MLflow
